
# Exuberance 🫀
You want to live, but you're just power flowing through a body.

**Become one with Redstone dust** and harbor its potential inside of you. Release your inner energy using various esoteric weaponry!


## Power of Redstone
To get Redstone in your veins, you need to become Redstone Being. A ritual that replaces your human blood with Redstone will unlock your **potential (IVF)** and turn you into a Being.

Your *potential* is separated into 3 independent axis - **Index, Vector, Factor.** They're stored inside of you and remain unchanged through time passing and your death.

To interact with these values, you'll need to use **Matrix Function Weapons** - special Redstone weapons that fluctuate the power within you.
